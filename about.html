About the studio

Stitched slowly, on purpose.

Purl & Paws makes crochet animals for people who want a gift that still shows the hand that made it.

Our mission

Make plush animals that feel chosen for one person — sturdy enough to be hugged, detailed enough to keep, and slow enough that the yarn still shows.

We care more about a good snout and a mane that stands up than about filling a warehouse. If a dragon needs another evening of scales, it gets another evening.

The story

The studio began in 2023 as a kitchen-table habit: one skein, one animal, then another, until the shelf held bears, barn birds, and a dragon that took a whole weekend. Purl & Paws still works that way. A creature is designed, crocheted in a small batch, and photographed before it leaves the table.

This season’s menagerie is fifteen animals, from a pocket-sized frog to a highland cow buried in fluffy yarn. Prices follow the time and fiber in each piece. A simple chicken takes less yarn than a maned lion or a unicorn with a specialty-fiber mane.

Stuffing is fiberfill. Faces are embroidered or finished with safety eyes. No two manes fall in quite the same direction, and that is part of the point.
