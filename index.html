Soft animals, stitched by hand.

Purl & Paws is a small storefront of plush friends — bears, foxes, dragons, and a highland cow with a coat you can get lost in. Every animal is crocheted one loop at a time.

The studio

A small shop for slow gifts.

The catalog stays short on purpose. Fifteen animals, each with its own shape, palette, and a few quirks left in the stitches. They are made to be held, gifted, and set somewhere you will actually see them.

  • 15 animals The full menagerie, from a pocket frog to a winged dragon.
  • Small batch Crocheted by hand in yarn picked for that creature.
  • Gift ready Quiet woodland friends, barnyard pals, and a few myths.
